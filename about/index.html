About AdonisIQ

Built by Josh Strauch, a solo founder and lifter.

AdonisIQ started from a simple frustration: collecting more fitness data was not the same thing as knowing what to do with it.

Why I built it

I wanted one place that could look across training, nutrition, recovery, body composition and progress together—then help turn those signals into a clearer next decision for the goal I was actually pursuing.

That became AdonisIQ: an AI physique and fitness coach built for people who are already putting in the work and want more useful feedback from the data they track.

I’m still building it with the same standard I wanted as a user: specific, practical, transparent about uncertainty, and focused on helping you make the next decision—not just giving you another dashboard.
