You’re tracking everything. But is anything actually coaching you?

You've got workout history. Calorie and protein targets. A wearable that tracks sleep. Maybe HRV, resting heart rate, body measurements and progress photos, too.

Yet the real question shows up tomorrow morning: Should I push harder, pull back, eat more, or change the plan?

Collecting numbers is easy. Turning those numbers into a useful next decision is the hard part. That is the gap AdonisIQ was built to close.

Meet Coach Adonis: feedback with a reason behind it

AdonisIQ brings together the training, nutrition and recovery information you log and the available data you connect, so Coach Adonis can provide more contextual coaching feedback. Rather than looking at one weigh-in or one tough workout in isolation, you can see how your recent patterns fit together—and why a recommendation makes sense.

That doesn't make every recommendation infallible. It makes the reasoning easier to understand and the next step easier to evaluate.

Adonis Forge: stop guessing what to train next

A good training plan has to fit your equipment, schedule and experience—not just look good on paper. Adonis Forge supports customized programs, equipment profiles, exercise-specific set prescriptions, rep targets and RIR/RPE, workout history and deload planning.

Change the equipment available for a session, follow your prescribed sets and review performance over time. Progressive overload becomes something you can track and judge—not an instruction to add weight blindly every workout.

Already serious about lifting? Forge can help you keep the structure without losing flexibility. Just starting? It gives you a clearer place to begin.

Recovery belongs in the plan—not in the rearview mirror

Training hard is important. Knowing when your recovery isn't keeping up is important too. AdonisIQ's recovery views incorporate available sleep and physiological trends, such as HRV and resting heart rate, alongside other training context.

One poor night of sleep isn't an automatic command to skip the gym. A sustained pattern of poor sleep, unusual fatigue and declining performance is worth taking seriously. AdonisIQ helps surface context for those choices; it does not diagnose medical conditions or guarantee that a readiness score captures everything.

Calories and macros should serve your goal

Whether you're building muscle, losing fat or working toward body recomposition, training and nutrition have to work together. AdonisIQ helps you track nutrition targets and check-ins in the context of your larger goal rather than treating food logging as a separate chore.

What matters isn't a single perfect day. It's whether your calories, protein, training and recovery patterns support progress over time.

One place to understand your next move

Instead of mentally stitching together your training log, food tracker and sleep metrics, use AdonisIQ's Today experience to review the information that matters and Coach Adonis to help interpret it.

No app replaces consistency, judgment or qualified professional care. But your fitness tools should make the work you already do more useful.
