Where Is Heart Rate Recovery
on Apple Watch? Exact Taps

Find Cardio Recovery on iPhone, check your post-workout heart rate on Apple Watch, troubleshoot missing values and understand what the trend can—and cannot—tell you.

By AdonisIQ EditorialOctober 15, 20269–12 min read

Fast answer: On a recent iPhone, open Health → Search → Heart → Cardio Recovery. Some older instructions call the Search tab Browse, and older systems may use the name Heart Rate Recovery. On Apple Watch, open the Heart Rate app after a recorded workout and look for Post-Workout if your watchOS version shows it. To review a saved workout on iPhone, open Fitness → Sessions → your workout → Heart Rate and expand the details where available. These are related views, not always identical one-minute measurements.

Key takeaways

  • Health calls the historical metric Cardio Recovery in newer iOS versions; older guides may say Heart Rate Recovery.
  • Apple Watch continues measuring heart rate for three minutes after you end a workout. Wear it during the recovery period.
  • No reading does not automatically mean your sensor or permissions are broken. Workout type, measurement coverage and the relevant Health metric's eligibility all matter.
  • The widely cited 12-bpm threshold comes from clinical exercise-testing research. It is not a universal Apple Watch pass/fail score.
  • Compare similar workouts and overall recovery. Do not add or remove lifting sets solely from one post-workout heart-rate number.

How do I find heart rate recovery on Apple Watch after a workout?

Immediately after a workout: watchOS 10 and newer

  1. Record an exercise session with the Workout app.
  2. When finished, swipe right, then tap End.
  3. Keep wearing your Watch for the next three minutes. Apple continues collecting heart rate to measure post-workout recovery.
  4. In the workout summary, use the heart icon if present to see your current heart rate during recovery. You can also open the Heart Rate app, turn the Digital Crown and look for a Post-Workout section if available on your version.
  5. For older sessions and a broader trend, switch to the iPhone Health or Fitness app instead of trying to reopen an old workout from the Watch's Workout launch screen.
Don't confuse the apps: the Workout app records the activity; the Heart Rate app can show recent post-workout heart-rate data. The iPhone Health app provides the historical Cardio Recovery metric, while the Fitness app lets you inspect a specific workout.

What changed on watchOS 9 and earlier?

Apple introduced its Health app Cardio Recovery metric with watchOS 9/iOS 16. Earlier Apple interfaces and third-party tutorials often use the name Heart Rate Recovery. On older Apple Watch software, check the Heart Rate app's Recovery or Post-Workout view after recording an exercise session. Avoid relying on screenshots from watchOS 8 or 9 to navigate much newer layouts, as the workout summary interface has changed.

How do I find Cardio Recovery in the iPhone Health app?

  1. Open Health on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Search in the bottom navigation (older versions: Browse).
  3. Tap Heart.
  4. Scroll to and tap Cardio Recovery. You can also use Health's search field to search directly for that metric.
  5. Use the chart time-range controls to inspect available trends. If the page has no entries, start with the checks below.

You can also open Fitness → Sessions → a recorded workout, then look for Heart Rate and tap Show More where available. The Fitness workout trace is useful when you want to inspect a particular session rather than an aggregate Cardio Recovery series.

Can I pin Cardio Recovery to my Health Summary?

Open the Cardio Recovery detail page and look for Pin to Summary if that option is offered in your iOS version. You can also open Health → Summary → Edit to manage pinned metrics. Apple has changed these controls across releases; not every data type is pinnable on every version. Avoid following older instructions that assume every metric has an “Add to Favorites” star.

Why is heart rate recovery missing or blank?

There isn't one universal reason. The Watch records heart-rate samples and Apple can derive recovery-related information from supported data, but you should not expect a Cardio Recovery entry after every possible workout. The workout graph and Health Cardio Recovery metric are not interchangeable displays.

Check whether heart rate measurement is enabled

On Apple Watch, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Heart Rate and verify that Heart Rate is enabled. Apple's Watch user guide also documents checking heart-rate settings in the iPhone Watch app under My Watch → Privacy. Heart Rate and Fitness Tracking are separate controls; there is no general dedicated “turn on HRR” switch in Workout view settings.

Check Watch fit and the recovery window

A securely fitted Watch with contact above the wrist bone can improve optical tracking. Movement, poor sensor contact, tattoos, skin temperature and certain exercises can affect readings. After finishing a workout, keep the Watch on during the following minutes rather than immediately removing it to shower or charge.

Should you walk for 60–90 seconds before tapping End?

A short cool-down can be useful for comfort and safety after harder workouts, but it is not a guaranteed fix for a missing Cardio Recovery value. The timing matters: if you walk for two minutes before pressing End, you have changed the exercise-ending heart rate and the subsequent recovery comparison. For meaningful trends, follow a similar workout-ending and cool-down protocol each time. Do not abruptly stop high-intensity exercise just to chase a particular watch number.

What is a good heart rate recovery number?

Heart rate recovery (HRR) usually means how far your heart rate falls over a defined period after exercise, often one minute. As an example, a heart rate falling from 150 bpm to 126 bpm over one minute represents a 24-bpm drop. In a standardized context, larger declines commonly suggest faster parasympathetic reactivation. However, the starting rate, exercise intensity, active or passive recovery, medication use and how HRR is calculated all affect interpretation.

Why the “12 bpm is normal” rule can mislead

The frequently cited 12-bpm cutoff comes from a 1999 study by Cole and colleagues involving treadmill stress testing and a specified cool-down protocol. In that study, a one-minute drop of 12 bpm or less identified a group with greater long-term risk. It was not designed as a universal fitness score for individual Apple Watch workouts. Other studies and test protocols use different recovery intervals and thresholds.

A result below 12 bpm on a consumer Watch is not a diagnosis, just as a result above 20 bpm does not certify excellent heart health. A repeated unusual response, particularly with exercise intolerance or other symptoms, is worth discussing with a healthcare professional. Chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness or other concerning acute symptoms require prompt medical attention rather than self-interpreting a wearable statistic.

Does heart rate recovery work for weightlifting?

The Watch can track heart rate during a recorded strength-training workout, and post-workout samples may still be available. It is not accurate to say strength modes never record HRR or that a continuous steady-state heart rate is an absolute requirement. But lifting poses interpretation challenges: exercise comes in short sets, rests are variable, upper-body wrist movements may disturb optical readings, and the final set may differ dramatically from a continuous run or bike ride.

If you want to follow a consistent HRR trend, compare sessions with similar ending intensity and recovery conditions—such as comparable conditioning or cardio workouts—rather than mixing a heavy squat day with a leisurely walk and assuming the difference reflects readiness.

Can I turn heart rate recovery on?

There is no standalone recovery switch you need to flip for every workout. Check that heart-rate monitoring is enabled, that the workout is recorded properly, and that the sensor captures useful heart-rate data. If your iPhone Health page remains empty, inspect individual workouts in Fitness and data sources in Health. Restarting or updating devices is a reasonable general troubleshooting step, but don't unpair your Watch or revoke app permissions as a first resort.

If you use AdonisIQ, review the app's current supported Apple Health integrations and permissions. Do not assume that granting basic Apple Health permission automatically means every HealthKit quantity—including one-minute HRR—is imported or used by a specific coaching feature.

How should you use heart rate recovery in training?

Think of HRR as one contextual signal rather than a control that dictates your next set. It's most informative when compared with similar sessions over time. A one-off drop may change because of recent exercise intensity, temperature, hydration, sleep, caffeine, medications or differences in cooldown behavior.

Review your training performance and how you feel alongside the recovery trend. If repeated comparable sessions show slower-than-usual recovery and your sleep, fatigue and performance are worsening, it may be sensible to review workload and general health. There is no validated “two bad HRR weeks = mandatory deload” rule, and a strong post-workout HRR value does not automatically justify adding lifting volume.

Frequently asked questions

Where is heart rate recovery on Apple Watch?

After a recorded workout, open the Heart Rate app and look for Post-Workout if your software displays that section. Apple's current Workout summary may also provide a heart icon for live post-workout heart rate. For historical numbers, use iPhone Health and Fitness.

Where is Cardio Recovery on iPhone?

Open Health → Search (or Browse) → Heart → Cardio Recovery. For a specific session, check Fitness → Sessions → workout → Heart Rate and expand the graph where available.

Why does my Apple Watch show no heart rate recovery?

The Watch may not have enough post-workout samples, the optical reading may be noisy, or the session may not produce a Cardio Recovery entry. Confirm heart-rate tracking is enabled and compare across properly saved workouts. A missing value alone does not establish a permission failure.

What is a normal one-minute heart rate recovery by age?

There is no single universally accepted Apple Watch cutoff by age. A 12-bpm threshold appears in a particular treadmill-testing study, but interpretation depends on the protocol, health context, medications, fitness and recovery method. Compare similar sessions and seek medical advice for persistent concerns.

Does Apple Watch measure heart rate recovery during strength training?

It can record workout and post-workout heart-rate samples during strength exercises, but the trend may be hard to interpret because lifting and rest periods vary. Do not assume every strength session generates a Cardio Recovery entry.

How do I enable Cardio Recovery?

There is no separate universal HRR toggle. Verify Watch Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Heart Rate is enabled, record a workout, and continue wearing the Watch for post-workout measurements. Cardio Recovery availability depends on supported data and software.
