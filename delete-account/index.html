Account & privacy

Delete your AdonisIQ account

Request deletion of your AdonisIQ account and associated personal data. You can make a request here even if you no longer have the app installed.

Request account and data deletion

To request deletion, email us from the address associated with your AdonisIQ account if possible. We'll confirm account ownership when necessary before processing the request.

Email account deletion request → admin@adonisiq.app

If the button doesn't open your email app, send an email to the address above with the subject “AdonisIQ Account Deletion Request.” Include the email associated with your account. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, medical records, or identity documents in the initial message.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open AdonisIQ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps shown.

If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app, use the email request above. Reinstalling the app is not required.

What happens after your request?

  1. We may ask you to verify that the account belongs to you.
  2. We process the deletion request and address the account data held by AdonisIQ and relevant service providers.
  3. We'll respond to your email with an update or confirmation when the request has been handled.

Deletion is intended to be permanent; please save any information you want to keep before proceeding.

What information is covered?

An account deletion request covers your AdonisIQ account and associated personal data under our control, including information such as:

  • Account and profile details;
  • Workout history, coaching conversations and training plans;
  • Nutrition logs, check-ins, body measurements and progress photos;
  • Health and recovery data you submitted or authorized us to store.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. Such records are retained only where necessary and are subject to our Privacy Policy. Information held independently in Apple Health, Android Health Connect, or your device is not deleted from those services by deleting your AdonisIQ account.

Subscriptions are managed separately

Deleting your AdonisIQ account or uninstalling the app does not automatically cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel an active subscription through the store where you purchased it to avoid future renewals.

  • Manage Apple App Store subscriptions
  • Manage Google Play subscriptions

You may request account deletion without first canceling. We can help with account-related questions, but billing and cancellation are handled by the applicable app store.

Need a different privacy request?

If you want to ask about a specific record, request deletion of Android waitlist information, or have a privacy question without deleting your entire account, contact admin@adonisiq.app. See our Privacy Policy and Support Center for more information.
