AdonisIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Contact: adonisiqadmin@gmail.com

AdonisIQ ("we", "us") provides an AI-powered health and fitness coaching app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using AdonisIQ you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address (or Apple ID via Sign in with Apple) and authentication details.
  • Health & fitness data you enter: body weight, waist and body measurements, daily check-ins (sleep, energy, soreness, stress, mood), nutrition, workouts, and optional lab biomarkers.
  • Apple Health data (with your permission): step count and resting heart rate, read only to calculate your recovery and consistency scores. This data is never used for advertising and is not shared with third parties for their own use.
  • Usage data: basic app interactions needed to operate and improve the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core features: your AdonisIQ Score, readiness, adaptive targets, trends, and AI coaching.
  • To generate personalized coaching. Your logged data is sent to our AI provider(s) solely to produce coaching responses for you. It is not used to train third-party models for advertising.
  • To operate your account, process your subscription, and provide support.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app (secure cloud hosting/database and the AI language-model provider that generates coaching), and only as needed to deliver the service, and with Apple for subscription processing.

Data Retention & Security

We retain your data while your account is active. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your Choices & Rights

  • You can revoke Apple Health access anytime in the iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Medical Disclaimer

AdonisIQ is for general fitness and informational purposes only and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. Consult a qualified physician regarding any health concern or before acting on lab values.

Children

AdonisIQ is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
