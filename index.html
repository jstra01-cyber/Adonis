Built by a lifter

Made because tracking more wasn’t the answer.

AdonisIQ was built by Josh Strauch, a solo founder and lifter who wanted clearer answers from the training, nutrition and recovery data he was already collecting.

The goal is simple—connect the information you’re already collecting and turn it into clearer decisions about training, nutrition, recovery and progress.

Questions before you try it

The things skeptical lifters should ask.

AdonisIQ should earn your trust by being specific about what it does, what it costs and how it uses your data.

How is this different from the AI summaries in other apps?

AdonisIQ is built to connect the signals you already track—training, nutrition, recovery, body composition and progress—before giving you a recommendation. The goal is not another recap of what happened. It is a clearer next move, with context for why.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

If you keep Premium, the full AdonisIQ experience is $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year in the U.S. App Store. You can manage or cancel the subscription through your App Store subscription settings.

Does AdonisIQ replace a personal trainer or medical professional?

No. AdonisIQ is a fitness and physique coaching tool. It can help organize your training, nutrition, recovery and progress data, but it does not replace medical care or a qualified professional when you need one.

What data can AdonisIQ use?

Depending on what you choose to connect or enter, AdonisIQ can use training, nutrition, recovery, weight and body-composition trends, check-ins, progress photos and supported Apple Health data. Android Health Connect support is being built for the Android release.

What if I use a GLP-1?

AdonisIQ can account for GLP-1 use as coaching context—for example by emphasizing protein, training performance, recovery and whether intake is becoming too difficult to sustain. It does not recommend medication dosing.

Do you sell my health or fitness data?

No. AdonisIQ does not sell your personal data and does not use your health or fitness information for third-party advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
