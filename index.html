Before you begin

Questions, answered.

Is AdonisIQ a workout tracker or an AI coach?

Both: you can track your workouts, nutrition and progress, while Coach Adonis uses your logged history to explain patterns and suggest practical next steps.

What does AdonisIQ use from Apple Health?

With permission, supported health and fitness measurements can include activity, sleep and recovery-related metrics as available on your device. You choose what to share. Availability varies by platform, permissions and connected devices.

Is Recovery Readiness the same as the AdonisIQ Score?

No. The AdonisIQ Score reflects goal consistency; Recovery Readiness reflects available recovery signals. A high score in one does not guarantee a high score in the other.

Can I use AdonisIQ if I take a GLP-1 medication?

Yes. AdonisIQ can provide general fitness and nutrition context focused on adequate protein, training and recovery. It does not prescribe or recommend medication dosing, and does not replace medical care.

Is AdonisIQ available for Android?

Android access is in development. Visit the Android early access page for the current release status. iPhone users can access AdonisIQ through the App Store.

What happens after the trial?

If you're eligible and begin a trial, it converts to the subscription you selected unless canceled before renewal. Apple displays your specific trial eligibility and terms prior to purchase.
