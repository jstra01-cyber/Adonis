Privacy Policy

AdonisIQ · Last updated: June 1, 2026

AdonisIQ ("we", "us") provides an AI-assisted health and physique coaching app. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using AdonisIQ you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Health & fitness data you enter or connect: daily check-ins (sleep, mood, energy, stress, body weight, waist), daily step counts (including data you choose to import from Apple Health), training logs, nutrition entries, goals, and lab/biomarker results.
  • Subscription data: purchase and entitlement status processed through Apple/Google and our billing provider (RevenueCat). We never receive your full payment card details.

How we use your information

  • To calculate your metrics (e.g., TDEE, macro targets, readiness) and generate coaching guidance and reports.
  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, we read your step count from Apple Health solely to display and track your daily activity in the app. This data is not used for advertising and is not shared. You can revoke access anytime in the iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

AI processing

To generate explanations, summaries, and coaching, relevant, de-identified context from your entries may be processed by third-party AI providers under contractual confidentiality. We do not sell your data or use it to train third-party public models.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers who help us operate the app (cloud hosting, AI processing, and subscription management) under agreements that require them to protect it, and when required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile > Delete account in the app. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and hashed credentials. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

AdonisIQ is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Medical disclaimer

AdonisIQ provides informational fitness and wellness guidance only and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified physician regarding your health and before acting on lab-related insights.

Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data. Contact us to exercise these rights.
